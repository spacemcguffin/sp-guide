Eric Theodore Cartman, commonly known as Cartman, is one of the four main boys in the animated television series South Park, created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone.

Known for his manipulative schemes, outrageous behaviour and distinctive voice, Cartman is a central figure in many of the show's storylines. This prototype demonstrates how longer, encyclopaedic entries could look alongside the existing South Park Guide news pages.

Background

Cartman lives in South Park, Colorado, with his mother, Liane Cartman. His adventures frequently involve Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski and Kenny McCormick.

Personality

Cartman is portrayed as selfish, impulsive and frequently antagonistic, particularly toward Kyle. His elaborate plans often drive the plot of an episode.
