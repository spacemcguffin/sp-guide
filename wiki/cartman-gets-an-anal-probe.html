“Cartman Gets an Anal Probe” is the first episode of the American animated television series South Park. It introduces Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, Eric Cartman and Kenny McCormick.

The episode follows the boys as strange events unfold in South Park, including an alien encounter and the disappearance of Kyle's younger brother, Ike.

Plot

After Cartman describes a bizarre dream involving aliens, the boys begin to suspect that something unusual has happened to him. Meanwhile, Kyle is worried about Ike, and the group becomes involved in a series of encounters with visitors from outer space.

Production and broadcast

The episode was written and directed by series creators Trey Parker and Matt Stone and first aired on Comedy Central on 13 August 1997. It established the cutout-animation look and irreverent humour associated with the series.

Characters

Principal characters include Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, Eric Cartman and Kenny McCormick. The episode also introduces several supporting residents of South Park.

Trivia

  • This is the series premiere of South Park.
  • It was produced using a combination of traditional cutout animation and computer animation.
