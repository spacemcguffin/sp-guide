Overview

“Cartman Gets an Anal Probe” is the first episode of the American animated television series South Park. It introduces Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski, Eric Cartman and Kenny McCormick, and establishes the series’ mix of surreal comedy and social satire.

The episode follows the boys as strange events unfold in South Park, including an alien encounter and the disappearance of Kyle's younger brother, Ike.

Plot

Cartman recounts a bizarre dream about aliens, leading his friends to suspect that he has experienced an extraterrestrial encounter. Meanwhile, Kyle becomes worried when his younger brother Ike goes missing.

As the boys investigate, they become caught up in an increasingly strange series of events involving alien visitors.

Production

The episode premiered on Comedy Central on 13 August 1997. Its cut-out-inspired animation established the visual style that would become synonymous with the series.
