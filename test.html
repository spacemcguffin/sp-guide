Character Guide

Kenny McCormick

South Park's perpetually doomed, orange-parka-wearing fourth-grader.

Kenny McCormick is one of the four central characters of South Park, alongside Stan Marsh, Kyle Broflovski and Eric Cartman. Recognisable by his orange parka and usually muffled voice, Kenny has been part of the series since its beginning.

He became particularly famous for repeatedly dying during the show's early seasons, usually followed by Stan and Kyle's familiar reaction to his latest demise.

About Kenny

The quiet one

Kenny is the poorest member of the boys' group and lives with the McCormick family on the outskirts of South Park. His family's financial situation has been a recurring part of the character since the earliest seasons.

Although his parka usually obscures his speech, Kenny's personality has become considerably more developed over the course of the series.

Appearance

The orange parka

Kenny is almost always seen wearing his distinctive orange hooded parka, which covers most of his face and muffles his speech.

On the occasions when the hood comes off, Kenny has blond hair. His uncovered appearance was kept relatively rare during the show's earlier years.

Kenny's deaths

Oh my God, they killed Kenny!

Kenny's repeated deaths became one of the defining running jokes of early South Park. He could be crushed, burned, electrocuted, shot or otherwise killed, only to return in a later episode without explanation.

? Deaths and counting

The running gag eventually became less frequent, while later stories explored why Kenny is seemingly unable to remain dead.
