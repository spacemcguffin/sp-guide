RIP Kenny

The Season 29 opener was explosive.

Cartman, Kenny, Stan and Kyle returned in the explosive Season 29 premiere, South American Biker Gangs.

The episode concluded following the sad death of Kenny, whose e-bike exploded after talking to Randy and the other adult bikers. Whether or not this will have ramifications for the rest of the season remains to be seen.

This is Kenny's first death since South Park: The End of Obesity, and the first Kenny death within a regular episode since the Season 22 episode Bike Parade.

The next episode is due to air Wednesday September 30.
