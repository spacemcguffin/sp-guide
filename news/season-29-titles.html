Season 29 Debuts New Title Sequence

Come on down to South America...

South Park's 29th season has a brand new title sequence, for the rebranded South America.

A revised title sequence has debuted with the launch of Season 29. The town has been subtly updated to include new locations and sixth-graders on their e-bikes. Donald Trump also makes a cameo near the end of the sequence, drying himself off using the American flag.
