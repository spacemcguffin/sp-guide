Season 29 Begins September 16th

New episodes are on the way!

South Park's 29th season will begin September 16th.

Press Release

Comedy Central and Paramount+ today announced that the 29th season of the renowned series, South Park, will premiere Wednesday, September 16th at 10pm ET/PT on Comedy Central. New episodes will be available to stream on Paramount+ around the world, with next-day availability in the U.S., Canada and Australia.

Additional new episodes will premiere on Wednesdays: September 30, October 14, October 28, November 11, and November 25.
