Little Randy Joins the South Park Boys

The Fourth Grade has a new kid

After a visit to the Backrooms of Sephora in You Like That on Your Face, Randy Marsh de-aged into a fourth grade version of himself. This week, in Guinea Pig on a Stick, Little Randy cemented himself as a recurring presence and joined the main boys on their mission.

After years of Tegridy Farms, this new version of Randy is a refreshing spin on the character. At this stage, it feels like Little Randy may be a mainstay for the remainder of Season 29, at least.

The next episode is due to air Wednesday November 11.
