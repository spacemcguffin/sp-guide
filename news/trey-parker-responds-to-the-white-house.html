Trey Parker Responds to the White House, Following Season 27 Premiere

Sermon on the 'Mount seemed to upset Donald Trump.

South Park creators Trey Parker and Matt Stone appeared at Comic-Con International in San Diego after Season 27 premiere aired with Sermon on the 'Mount.

The White House issued the following statement, following the episode's premiere.

"This show hasn't been relevant for over 20 years and is hanging on by a thread with uninspired ideas in a desperate attempt for attention. President Trump has delivered on more promises in just six months than any other president in our country's history - and no fourth-rate show can derail President Trump's hot streak."

When asked about the White House's reaction during the Comic Con panel, Parker joked:

"We're terribly sorry."
