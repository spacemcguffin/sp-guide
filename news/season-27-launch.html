July 2, 2025

Season 27 Pushed Back to July 23, Matt & Trey Respond: “Shitshow”

The boys from South Park will return, just a little later than expected

Season 27 was originally slated to premiere Wednesday, July 9. However, Comedy Central today announced that South Park’s 27th season premiere has been pushed back to Wednesday, July 23.

Trey Parker & Matt Stone

In response to the press release from Comedy Central about the change in premiere date for Season 27, Trey Parker and Matt Stone responded:

"This merger is a shitshow and it’s fucking up South Park. We are at the studio working on new episodes and we hope the fans get to see them somehow."
